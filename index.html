The approach

Respect the craft. Understand the science. Design for the long term.

At Tesla Nova, we bridge the gap between historical craftsmanship and advanced atomic science. We specialize in the curation, restoration, and safe handling of antique nuclear and radiologically significant artifacts. Our mission is to preserve the physical history of the atomic age while providing rigorous scientific verification and measurement for collectors, researchers, and institutions.

Every artifact we handle undergoes strict radiological assessment to ensure modern safety compliance, honoring both the heritage of early atomic discovery and the uncompromising standards of modern science.
